FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still have questions? Write to the team at investors@sidanaventures.com.

After signing an agreement, how long does Sidana Ventures work with startups?

Our standard agreement is for 24 months (2 years), during which we actively support founders in securing multiple rounds of funding as per their business needs.

How does Sidana Ventures bring the right investors for startups?

We have a strong network of global investors, family offices, venture capitalists and angel groups across diverse sectors. By understanding each founder's requirements, we connect them with investors who specialise in their industry, ensuring the right strategic fit and funding support.

After onboarding, how much time does Sidana Ventures take to connect startups with investors?

Once a startup is onboarded, our team typically takes 4 to 6 weeks to prepare founders with pitch readiness, compliance and financial structuring before connecting them with suitable investors.

How many companies has Sidana Ventures successfully funded?

Sidana Ventures has facilitated funding for numerous startups. See the portfolio on our Startups page.

Why should startups choose Sidana Ventures?

Sidana Ventures offers startups access to a global network of investors, angel groups and venture capitalists. We believe fundraising is not an endpoint but a step towards long-term success. Our team works closely with founders at every stage, providing guidance, mentorship and strategic support to build their growth path and achieve their business goals.
